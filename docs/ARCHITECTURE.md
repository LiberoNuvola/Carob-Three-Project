# Carob Three 2026 — Architecture

## Purpose

Carob Three investigates whether heterogeneous existing technologies can be coordinated into one testable ecological machine without creating a new general-purpose protocol stack.

## Functional loop

```text
Ecosystem
   ↓
Human observers + plant/environment sensors
   ↓
Data capture and provenance
   ↓
AI / ML interpretation
   ↓
Hypothesis
   ↓
Evidence + community/scientific validation
   ↓
Accepted knowledge
   ↓
Contributor recognition / optional reward
   ↓
Policy decision
   ↓
Ecological intervention or autonomous action
   ↓
Ecosystem
   ↺
```

## Ownership boundary

Carob Three owns:

- ecological domain semantics;
- evidence and validation rules;
- discovery workflow;
- knowledge-state transitions;
- closed-loop coordination policies.

Carob Three does not own or recreate:

- a blockchain runtime;
- a general AI runtime;
- a universal adapter;
- a general governance engine;
- generic sensor firmware;
- generic robotics infrastructure.

## Evidence rule

AI output is a hypothesis or classification, not scientific truth. Any transition from hypothesis to accepted knowledge requires explicit evidence criteria appropriate to the experiment.

## External systems

Existing systems should be composed whenever possible. An external system may provide identity, provenance, storage, settlement, rewards, governance, sensing, computation, or execution. Carob Three interacts with such systems only through explicit interfaces and policies.

## First experimental target

The first experiment should minimize infrastructure and maximize falsifiability: a small real ecosystem, heterogeneous observations, a transparent evidence trail, an AI hypothesis stage, and a human/scientific validation stage.
