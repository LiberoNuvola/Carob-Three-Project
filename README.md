# Carob Three

Open research project exploring AI-driven plant communication, citizen science, decentralized data, and autonomous ecological management.

## What is Carob Three?

Carob Three is a research and experimental project focused on a single functional system:

ecosystem observation → decentralized data → AI interpretation → evidence and community validation → formalized knowledge → contributor recognition → progressively autonomous ecological management.

The carob is the symbolic origin of the project, not its domain boundary. The architecture is intended to be species-agnostic and applicable to ecological and agricultural systems more broadly.

## 2026 position

Carob Three does not aim to replace existing sensor platforms, AI/ML frameworks, citizen-science systems, robotics, provenance systems, or blockchains.

The research question is whether these existing components can be coordinated into one coherent, testable machine.

The project prioritizes real-world experiments, evidence, interoperability, scientific validation, and reproducibility.

### Epistemic rule

**AI generates hypotheses; it does not establish truth.**

A candidate discovery must pass defined evidence and validation criteria before it can become accepted knowledge or influence autonomous policy.

## Architecture boundary

Carob Three owns the ecological domain model, evidence and validation policies, discovery workflow, and closed-loop coordination.

External infrastructure may provide sensing, computation, storage, provenance, identity, rewards, governance, or execution.

Carob Three remains independent of any specific blockchain or execution protocol.

External economic or execution systems, where used, are consumed only through explicit interfaces and policies; their internal logic does not become part of Carob Three.

## Research status

The original concept was developed in 2023. The 2026 project reopens it as an experimental research program while testing which parts are already established by prior art and which parts of the complete functional combination remain underexplored.

No claim of absolute novelty, invention, or patentability is made by this repository.
